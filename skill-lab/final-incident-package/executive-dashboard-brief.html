Business Impact

The dashboard's revenue data is stale: no new data has loaded since 2026-08-01, and the two most recent scheduled refreshes both failed completely. Separately, the raw data behind the dashboard has quality problems — a duplicate order record and an order with a negative revenue value — that would need to be corrected before the data could be trusted for reporting.

The scale of business impact (how much revenue reporting is affected, how many decisions relied on stale numbers) is not yet confirmed.

What We Know

  • The data feed failed quality validation: a duplicated order record, a missing required field on one order, and an order with a negative revenue value were all confirmed.
  • About a quarter of records in the underlying data are older than the required freshness window.
  • The daily refresh failed for two consecutive scheduled runs (2026-08-02 and 2026-08-03), loading zero new rows in either run.
  • The refresh failure traces to an upstream change: the source system began sending region names in a different format than the pipeline expects, starting 2026-08-02.
  • The pipeline automatically retried once and failed again identically — this is a persistent issue, not a temporary glitch.
  • The duplicate-order and negative-revenue issues are independent of the refresh failure and were never reached because the refresh halted earlier.

What We Do Not Know

  • Whether the upstream format change was intentional or itself a mistake — not yet confirmed by the team that owns that export.
  • Whether the one blank region value is related to the format change or a separate data-entry gap.
  • The business/financial scale of impact from two days of stale dashboard data.

Decision or Action Needed

No decision is required from leadership right now. The dashboard should remain blocked until (a) the upstream export format issue is resolved or the pipeline is updated to handle it, and (b) the duplicate-record and negative-revenue issues in the source data are corrected. Confirmation is needed from the team that owns the upstream data export on whether their format change was intentional.
