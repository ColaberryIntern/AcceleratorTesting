# 🎯 Advisor Gap Report

### The 45-minute conversation, in 45 seconds.

---

## 😩 The problem

One advisor. **200+ students a semester.**

A student brings in a résumé and a job they want.
The advisor reads both, works out what's missing, and writes out a plan.

It takes **an hour**. It's the best work they do.
And there is only ever time for a handful of students.

---

## ✨ What it does

Drop in a résumé. Drop in the job posting.

Get back a report that says:

- 🔍 **What's missing** — and the exact line of the posting that proves it
- 🚀 **What to build** — real projects, ranked by how fast they close the gap
- 🗓️ **A six-week plan** — about six hours a week

**In under a minute.**

---

## 🛡️ Why you can trust it

Every claim is quoted from the real posting.
Every project comes from a catalog **your team approved.**

Nothing reaches an advisor until it passes a fixed checklist.

> **If it can't prove it, it doesn't print it.**

---

## 👥 Who needs this

**Community college career services.**
Two-person teams carrying hundreds of students.

The advisors who already know exactly what to say —
they just can't say it two hundred times.

---

## 💡 Why it matters

> **A student's next job shouldn't depend on whether there was time in the calendar.**

---

## 📈 What changes

|  | Today | With this |
|---|---|---|
| ⏱️ | An hour per student | Under a minute |
| 👤 | A handful get the deep version | Every student does |
| 📋 | Quality varies with the day | Same checks, every time |
| 🤝 | Advisor spends the hour analysing | Advisor spends it *advising* |
